Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · KuzoStudiosPH, Philippines · kuzostudiosph@gmail.com

Keyboard Wizards is a single-player typing card battler with online leaderboards and asynchronous "ghost" duels. This policy explains what information the game stores, why, and how you can have it removed. It is short because we collect very little.

The short version

  • We do not collect your real name, email, location, contacts or payment details.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising trackers, or sell or share data with anyone.
  • To make duels and leaderboards work, the game stores a display name, your duel deck, a summary of how you play (your "ghost") and your scores online, under an anonymous ID.
  • You can ask us to delete your online data at any time (see Deleting your data).

1. What the game stores online

The game uses Google Firebase (Firestore and anonymous Authentication) to run duels and the online leaderboard. When you first go online, Firebase gives the game an anonymous account ID: a random identifier that is not linked to your email, Steam account or any personal details. Under that ID we store:

Your duel ghost (once you unlock duels and your ghost goes online):

  • the display name shown in duels (your Steam profile name on Steam, or the name you enter in the game elsewhere);
  • your duel deck (the list of cards);
  • your ghost's play style, summarised as numbers: typing speed, accuracy, how often and how quickly you counter, how often you combo, which cards you favour, how you play when your health is low, and when you use your legendary card;
  • your duel rating, and the number of duels your ghost has won and lost against others;
  • the game version, and when the ghost was last updated.

Other players see your display name, rating, rank, ghost speed and record, and fight your ghost in duels.

Run results (builds that use our online leaderboard, such as the web version): your display name, typing speed, points, rounds cleared, health lost, whether you won, and when.

Round progress (the same builds only): for each round you play, the run it belongs to, the round number, the enemy, and how many spells you cast and countered. We use this only to see where players get stuck, so we can balance the game.

Combined game statistics: anonymous running totals that are not tied to you, such as how many runs reached each monster, which cards are picked, and which legendaries win duels.

2. What Steam handles

On Steam, leaderboards and achievements are provided by Valve's Steamworks services and are covered by Valve's privacy policy, not this one. The game reads your Steam profile name so your ghost can carry it. We never receive your Steam password, email or payment information.

3. What stays on your computer

Your settings, progress, unlocked cards, Bestiary and similar save data are stored only on your device. The game also keeps a small local log of play events for debugging, which never leaves your computer.

4. Why we use this information

Only to run the game's online features (duels, ratings, leaderboards) and to understand how the game is played so we can fix bugs and balance it. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it or share it with third parties, other than the services that host it (Google Firebase, and Valve on Steam).

5. Where it is stored, and for how long

Online data is stored on Google Cloud servers through Firebase, which may be outside your country. Like any web service, Google's servers process the IP address of each request in order to answer it; we do not store IP addresses ourselves.

Ghost, rating and leaderboard data is kept while the game's online features run, unless you ask us to delete it. Combined statistics contain nothing personal and are kept as anonymous totals.

6. Deleting your data

Email kuzostudiosph@gmail.com with:

  • the display name you use in the game, and
  • roughly when you last played online (and whether on Steam or the web version).

We will remove your ghost, rating and leaderboard entries within 30 days. Because your online account is anonymous, these details are how we find it. You can also avoid storing new data by not opening duels; uninstalling the game removes the local save data.

7. Children

Keyboard Wizards is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Display names are chosen by players; please do not use your real name. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Contact us at kuzostudiosph@gmail.com and we will help.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and mention it in our Steam announcements or on Discord.
