Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · KuzoStudiosPH, Philippines · kuzostudiosph@gmail.com

Bayani: Auto Ascent is an auto-battler for iPhone, Android and PC. You can play it entirely offline; online play lets you climb the tower against other people. This policy explains what information the game stores, why, and how you can have it removed.

The short version

  • We do not collect your real name, email, contacts or payment details, and you don't make an account.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising trackers, or sell your data.
  • On phones, when you have an internet connection, the game signs in to our server with an anonymous ID made from your device as soon as it starts, and our server keeps your tickets and the record of how they changed.
  • Purchases are made through Apple or Google. When a purchase runs, Unity's purchasing service receives purchase and device information (see Purchases).
  • You can delete your online data at any time in the game: Settings > Delete online data.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, climb record, unlocked difficulties, and whether you own the Full Tower are stored only on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the game removes them.

2. What our server stores (online play)

Online play and tickets run on our own game server (Nakama, hosted by DigitalOcean in Singapore). On iPhone and Android, the first time the game starts with an internet connection, it creates an anonymous account from an identifier your device gives apps (so your daily tickets are ready even before you play online); it is not linked to your name, email, or Apple, Google or Steam account. Under it we store:

  • your tickets, and a record of each change (welcome tickets, daily tickets, a match entered, won or refunded, a ticket pack bought) with its date;
  • the date you last received daily tickets;
  • for each ticket pack you buy: the product, the store's transaction ID and date, and the store's confirmation, so the same purchase can never be counted twice.

During a match, your card picks pass through the server to the other players in that match, so every device can play the same fights. Matches themselves are not stored once they end. Other players see only a seat name and your picks in that match.

While the game is open on your screen, it keeps a connection to the server so the game can show how many players are online; the server only counts open connections and stores nothing about them. The connection closes when the game goes to the background.

Like any internet service, the server processes your device's IP address to answer it, and keeps short technical logs to keep the service running and to fix problems.

3. Purchases

The Full Tower and ticket packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play, under Apple's or Google's privacy policies. We never see your card or payment details. To deliver ticket packs, our server sends the store's receipt back to Apple or Google to confirm it.

The game handles purchases with Unity In-App Purchasing, a service of Unity Technologies. When a purchase runs, Unity receives information about the purchase (the product, price and transaction), device and app identifiers, technical details about your device (model, operating system, language), approximate location (country, from your IP address) and diagnostic data, to run the purchase service and prevent duplicate transactions. This is covered by Unity's privacy policy. The Unity engine the game is built with may also send Unity basic, non-identifying information about your device's hardware and software, under the same policy.

4. On Steam

On PC, the game and its achievements are provided through Valve's Steamworks services and are covered by Valve's privacy policy. We never receive your Steam password, email or payment information.

5. Why we use this information

Only to run online play and tickets, deliver what you buy, prevent cheating and duplicate purchases, and fix problems. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it. It is shared only with the services that run the game: DigitalOcean (our server's host), Apple or Google (purchases), and Unity (purchases).

6. Where it is stored, and for how long

Our server is in Singapore; Unity, Apple and Google may process data in other countries. We keep your online account, tickets and purchase records while the game's online play runs, or until you delete them. Server logs are kept for a short time only.

7. Deleting your data

In the game: Settings > Delete online data. This deletes your anonymous account, your tickets and their record from our server at once. Tickets cannot be recovered afterwards; the Full Tower stays yours, because it belongs to your Apple or Google account. We keep only a one-way scrambled form (a hash) of your device's identifier, which cannot be turned back into it, so that the welcome tickets are given once per device. You can also email kuzostudiosph@gmail.com and we will help. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.

8. Children

Bayani: Auto Ascent is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR, Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information, Korea's Personal Information Protection Act or the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Contact us at kuzostudiosph@gmail.com and we will help.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and mention it on our Discord.
