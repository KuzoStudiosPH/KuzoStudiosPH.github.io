Type to cast. Type faster to survive.

A card battler where every spell is cast by typing its name. Type to attack, type your enemy's incoming spell to counter it, and battle the monsters of Philippine folklore, all made in cut paper. Then duel other players' ghosts: the game learns how you play and sends your ghost to fight while you're away.
